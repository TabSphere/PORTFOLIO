CASE STUDY 03 · FREIGHT OPERATIONS

ShipOpz.

Connecting customer and operations journeys — one coherent platform for booking and freight operations.

Product · shipopz.comRole · Strategy, rebrand, UX directionType · Logistics platform

Visit live product ↗

The challenge

Freight booking lives on both sides of a counter: customers arranging shipments, and operations teams moving them. ShipOpz needed to bring those two worlds together in one coherent platform experience, rather than leaving them as disconnected tools and handoffs.

The work also arrived alongside a rebrand, so the platform’s structure, language and visual direction all had to move together.

The approach

I contributed strategy and UX direction across the booking, customer and operations journeys, keeping the focus on the handoffs — the points where a customer’s need becomes an operational task. Requirements and walkthroughs made those journeys concrete enough for delivery and testing.

  • Strategy and rebrand direction for the platform
  • UX direction across booking, customer and operations journeys
  • Requirements and walkthroughs that connect journeys to delivery
  • User workspace testing to validate the experience

My contribution

I contributed strategy, rebrand and UX direction for the booking, customer and operations journeys. I developed requirements and walkthroughs for the team and tested the user workspace, helping the platform grow into a single, understandable experience.

Focus: clarifying the handoffs between customer needs and operational workflows.
