CASE STUDY 02 · NFC & QR CUSTOMER ENGAGEMENT

tappivo.

A physical touchpoint. A digital connection. Turning taps and scans into useful business destinations.

Product · tappivo.comRole · Onboarding, workspaces, acceptance testingType · Customer engagement platform

Visit live product ↗

The challenge

Tappivo connects physical NFC and QR touchpoints — a card, a stand, a sticker — to useful digital destinations for businesses. The technology is simple for the customer, but the product behind it is not: every touchpoint must be ordered, provisioned, owned and pointed at the right destination.

The challenge was to make that chain understandable for everyone involved — the business owner managing their touchpoints, and the administrators keeping the whole system running.

The approach

I shaped the product around two clear workspaces and the journeys between them. Business onboarding gets owners started, owner workspaces keep their touchpoints and destinations manageable, and a super-admin workspace supports ordering, provisioning and oversight.

  • Business onboarding that explains the physical-to-digital journey
  • Owner workspaces for touchpoints and their destinations
  • Super-admin workspace for ordering, provisioning and support
  • Analytics concepts so owners can see engagement

My contribution

I shaped business onboarding, the owner and super-admin workspaces, touchpoint destinations and the ordering and provisioning concepts, and defined the analytics direction. I then validated the journeys through acceptance testing, so what shipped matched the agreed requirements.

Focus: making the journey from physical touchpoint to digital destination understandable.
