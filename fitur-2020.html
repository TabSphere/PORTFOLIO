FITUR 2020 / MADRID, SPAIN

Taking Ghana’s wildlife
story to Madrid.

In January 2020, I was selected to represent the Wildlife Division of Ghana’s Forestry Commission at FITUR in Madrid. It was an opportunity to speak about our protected areas with people exploring Ghana as a tourism destination.

When & where
22 to 26 January 2020
Madrid, Spain
My role
Assistant Public Relations Officer
Wildlife Division, Forestry Commission
Working alongside
Dr Richard Gyimah
Lead of our two-person delegation

AT THE GHANA STAND

A conversation, rather than just a brochure.

Our stand brought together posters, brochures and maps about Ghana’s protected areas. Dr Gyimah and I took turns meeting visitors, talking through the attractions and destinations, and sharing information about investment opportunities.

The printed materials gave us something concrete to show as we spoke. Documentaries about Mole National Park and other protected areas were also screened at Ghana’s stand, adding another way for visitors to discover the destinations.

LOOKING BEYOND OUR STAND

What I brought back from the experience.

The experience continued in the report Dr Gyimah and I prepared together. We looked at how the Commission could make better use of future fairs, from planning the exhibition materials earlier to showing destinations more clearly.

Our recommendations included stronger brochures and documentaries, a committee to coordinate the exhibits, and tablets to show visitors the places we were discussing. We also recommended developing investment proposals before approaching potential investors.
