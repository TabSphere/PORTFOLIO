I have shipped and led the delivery of digital products across SaaS, customer engagement and logistics. I connect user problems, design decisions and technical constraints, working with developers to turn requirements into usable products.

MY ROLE

Product definition, UX direction and UAT

01 / THE PROBLEM

Give candidates a clear starting point.

Structure preparation around the candidate’s experience and vacancy, with understandable onboarding and access.

02 / THE DECISION

Define the journey before the screens.

Connect CV and vacancy flows, define entitlements and include admin requirements alongside the candidate experience.

03 / THE VALIDATION

Make each journey testable.

Direct UX, validate flows through UAT and review release readiness against the product requirements.
